A direct connection.
A complete technology venture.

Kids Call Home gives children an easy way to call and message parent-approved family members from connected devices.

The opportunity

Family contact on the devices people already own.

Families need communication that is easy for a child and controlled by adults. Kids Call Home focuses on approved relationships, without public profiles or stranger discovery.

The operating model

Software and subscriptions.

The product combines cross-platform applications with backend systems and paid subscription options. Fluid’s role includes the venture and its US business operations.
